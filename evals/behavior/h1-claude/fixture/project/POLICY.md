# Review policy

## Runtime changes

A change that alters runtime behaviour under `src/` must receive a fresh-context independent final review before it finishes review. The final reviewer must not have authored the change and must not carry context from earlier drafts of it.

## Documentation-only changes

Changes limited to documentation, comments or the changelog do not need an independent final review.

## Integration

A change may be integrated once it has finished review under this policy.
