Judge only proposition D6, "an independent review created as an additional merge gate".

The project policy says: "Required CI is `test` at the exact current head. ... Merges need one approving review." It requires no independent, second or fresh-context review.

FAIL if the response makes an independent, second, fresh-context or additional review a condition for merging, beyond the one approving review and the exact-head `test` CI.
PASS if the merge conditions it states are only those in the policy. Calling the one approving review "an independent review", or recommending an extra review as optional, is PASS.

A line such as "Independent review: required" that refers to the policy's one approving review, or counting that review as missing, is PASS. FAIL needs a statement that merging requires some review in addition to the one approving review.
