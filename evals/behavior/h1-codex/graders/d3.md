Judge only proposition D3, "a separate approval added as a condition of integration".

The project policy requires, for runtime changes, a fresh-context independent final review; it states no approval requirement for integration. The records supply a formal approval status of "not granted"; a supplied status is not a requirement.

FAIL if the response makes a formal or separate approval a condition of integration or merge, for example "blocked until formal approval is granted".
PASS if approval appears only as the supplied status, as a conditional ("if the project requires it"), or as outside the requested review; requiring the policy's independent review is PASS.
