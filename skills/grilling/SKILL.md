# Grilling

Test important assumptions through adaptive questions. Reuse the user's goal, scope, answers and delegation. Follow live intent, not quoted triggers; a clear task change ends the interview while relevant decisions and open items remain.

## Keep the decision tree

Map decisions, required facts and assumptions as a **design tree**, kept as a small decision record in any format: settled items with their source, proposals, important open items with dependencies, explicit deferrals and unavailable prerequisites. Derive questions and closure from it.

Important choices belong to the user unless validly delegated. Broad delegation covers low-risk, reversible unlisted details within accepted constraints, not added material behavior, restrictions or tradeoffs; easy editing does not settle which. Make validly delegated choices without asking again.

Check accessible facts with permitted reads or non-mutating checks; ask only what the user must supply or decide. Hold a question that a pending or available fact check could change (its need, meaning, options or recommendation); independent ready ones proceed. Unknowns stay unknown; current code is not desired policy; an adopted assumption is not verified.

## Work one round

The **frontier** holds important unanswered questions with settled prerequisites. Probe assumptions, failure conditions and alternatives that could change the outcome; a sound plan may need none. Prioritize loss, rework and unblocking, then burden.

Advance the frontier with one fact or choice: one short question by default, a second only if independent and easy to answer together, within host and user limits. Values that vary and affect later reasoning independently are separate answer units; alternatives for one choice are one unit.

Ask about intended behavior at the user's demonstrated level. Test the draft with an imagined short reply settling one choice: if another requested decision stays unanswered, split it and ask only the first ready choice. Examples explain without requesting answers. Unselected ready units stay on the frontier; dependent follow-ups wait for their prerequisite. Prefer open questions until options are grounded. Ask with a question tool or numbered text within host rules, then wait and adapt.

## Recommend and update

Recommend from user criteria, checked facts or a concrete mechanism: the direct change, why it serves the aim, the material tradeoff. A starting value need not be optimal; give its bounded role and revisit condition. Check reasons and options against open premises in the record: a wider benefit needs other mechanisms or facts stated as its conditions, or keep to the direct effect; hypothetical conditions stay open until established or validly adopted.

Apply [replies](references/replies.md) to each user reply.

## Close from the record

Unavailable facts hold dependent steps; keep asking otherwise-ready questions about desired policy. If no independent step remains in scope, name the missing input and its owner. Pause when the user stops; waiting for an answer is an ongoing interview. Do not implement during the interview. Before closing, apply [closing](references/close.md).
