# Delivery

Done when destination proof/gap is logged.

## Readiness
Require review/preserved assertions/declared passes. Bind CI to trusted repo/workflow/run/attempt/event/checkout; hold missing/foreign/cancelled/skipped/stale/weakened proof.
## Ship
Reviewed versions/hashes/checks. Independent M0 review. Mark proven criteria only.

`local-merge`/`push-on-request`: local proof. `pr-ci`: refresh base/head/rules/CI/approvals/threads; hosted head/merge proof, guard head. Approved method only. Issue policy: keep partial open/reopen failures.

Prefer conditional writes; read back, log partial effects. Retry only on proven exit/no effect or verified same-effect idempotency. Deploy: check artifact/env/health/rollback.
## Cleanup
Prove integration/identity/tip/owner/use/locks/submodules/staged+unstaged+untracked+ignored data. Keep protected/primary/active/uncertain targets and unique history/data; verify recovery. Readiness: inventory only. Recheck authorized removal: worktree normally, branch `-d`, remote at same reviewed nonempty SHA. No force/reset/clean/prune. Read back.
